## Who we are

Kontrust Media builds advertising technology with the engineering discipline of space-grade software. Systems that fly can't afford to fail, stall, or waste a single cycle. We apply that standard to adtech, where every millisecond and every byte decides whether a bid is won or lost.

We are a **sub-millisecond optimization** company. We tune every layer of the stack until latency is measured in microseconds, not milliseconds.

## Our team

Our team comes from the **space industry** and has **many years of embedded engineering experience**. We grew up writing software for constrained hardware, where memory, power and time are limited, and where failure is not an option.

**Optimization is in our blood.** We bring the same mindset to adtech: lean code, predictable behavior, and no wasted cycles.

## What we believe

- **Performance is a feature.** We design for speed and efficiency from the first line of code, not as a late-stage patch.
- **Sub-millisecond by design.** Latency budgets are set up front and enforced at every layer.
- **Embedded mindset.** Years of working with tight resource limits taught us to respect every byte and every cycle.
- **Reliability by default.** Space-grade rigor means predictable behavior, strict correctness, and graceful handling of failure.
- **Optimization from the ground up.** From data structures and memory layout to network and infrastructure, every layer is built to be lean.
- **Simple, observable systems.** If we can't measure it, we can't improve it.
